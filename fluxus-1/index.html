The experience

Geometry becomes narrative. Space becomes puzzle.

Fluxus is an immersive 3D puzzle-adventure that transforms mathematical beauty into a navigable world of impossible spaces, recursive realities, and shifting dimensions.

Players manipulate portals, energy transmitters, and spatial connections to traverse richly detailed 360° environments that twist, wrap, scale, and reconnect through recursive transitions.

Mathematical art

Powered by Möbius geometry and the Riemann Sphere

The game turns conformal distortions, spherical inversions, logarithmic mappings, exponential warping, and Droste-style self-similarity into core mechanics. Instead of hiding mathematics behind the technology, Fluxus makes transformation its artistic language.
